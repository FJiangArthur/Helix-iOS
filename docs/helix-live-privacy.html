Privacy Policy — Helix Live

Effective 6 October 2026. Applies to the Helix Live app for Even G2 glasses distributed through Even Hub (package com.artjiang.helixconversate).

Summary

Helix Live shows live captions and short AI cues on your glasses during a conversation. It has no servers of its own. Audio and transcript text go directly from your phone to OpenAI, using your own OpenAI API key, only while a session is running. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used for analytics.

Permissions and why they are used

PermissionUse
g2-microphoneCaptures conversation audio from the glasses microphones while a session is running, to produce live captions and cues.
phone-microphoneOptional alternative audio source (you choose Glasses or Phone on the settings page). Same use as above.
network (api.openai.com only)Sends audio for transcription and transcript text for cue and answer generation to OpenAI. No other domain is contacted.

What data is processed

  • Audio — streamed to OpenAI's transcription API during a session. Helix Live does not record or store audio.
  • Transcript text — the last minute of conversation (and your optional Prep Note) is sent to OpenAI to generate cues and answers. Transcripts are kept only in memory for the running session.
  • Your OpenAI API key, Prep Notes and settings — stored only on your phone, in the Even Realities app's local storage for this app. They are never sent anywhere except the key, which is sent to api.openai.com to authorise your requests.

Third parties

OpenAI processes the audio and text you send under its own terms and privacy policy (openai.com/policies/privacy-policy), associated with your OpenAI account. The Even Realities app hosts Helix Live and relays glasses input and audio; see Even Realities' privacy policy for how it handles data.

Data retention and deletion

Helix Live keeps no data off your phone. Ending a session discards its transcript. To delete your key, Prep Notes and settings, clear them on the app's settings page or uninstall the app.

Children

Helix Live is not directed at children under 13.
