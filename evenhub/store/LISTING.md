## Short description
Live captions and AI conversation cues on your G2, powered by your own OpenAI key.

## Description
Helix Live listens to the conversation around you and shows it on your glasses:
- Live captions while people talk.
- Short AI cues that pop up and fade: explanations of terms, who someone is, and follow-up ideas.
- Answers when someone asks a question.
- Prep Notes: add context before a meeting so cues are relevant.
Hold the touchpad or R1 ring for the menu (pause, captions, cues, prep note, end). Double-tap to exit.
Requires your own OpenAI API key, entered on the app's phone page. Audio and text go only to OpenAI; nothing is stored off your phone.
